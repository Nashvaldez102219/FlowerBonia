FLOWERBONIA PRODUCT LAYOUT UPDATE

- Products are locked to a clean 2-column (2-by-2) shopping layout.
- Product grid is capped at 520px wide so cards do not become oversized on tablets/desktop.
- Product cards are shorter and more compact.
- Product image area, text, price, rating, favorite button, and cart button were reduced.
- Add to Cart and Favorites functionality are unchanged.

To apply to an existing project, replace:
lib/screens/products_page.dart
lib/widgets/product_card.dart

Then run:
flutter clean
flutter pub get
flutter run
